A quiet little word game, ready whenever you are.

How to play

Find the hidden word in 6 tries, or 10 tries for 7-letter mode. Choose a 4-, 5-, 6-, or 7-letter game from the top bar. Each guess must be a valid English word of the selected length.

When you first open the game each day, the selected length starts with that day's curated word. Press New word whenever you want a fresh word; it stays in place across reloads until you solve it, run out of guesses, or start another word.

The daily word is deterministic: the local calendar date is counted from a fixed 1 January 2024 epoch, then wrapped with modulo arithmetic over the selected length's curated answer list.

ACorrect — right letter, right place.
APresent — right letter, different place.
AAbsent — not in the word.

Use your keyboard or tap the letters below the board. Enter submits a guess; Backspace removes a letter.
