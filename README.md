# Infinite Wordle

Infinite Wordle is a simple browser word game with puzzles from 3 to 8 letters. The game is hosted on GitHub Pages — [play the game here](https://ashcx.github.io/infinite_wordle/).

## How to play

Infinite Wordle follows the standard Wordle rules: guess the hidden word and use the color clues to narrow it down. 
Choose from 3- to 8-letter words. The 3- to 6-letter modes give you 6 guesses; the 7- and 8-letter modes give you 10 guesses because the longer words are more difficult.
Complete at least 10 rounds with a win rate above 90% to unlock a special surprise.

1. Choose a word length from the menu at the top.
2. Enter a valid word using your keyboard or the on-screen keyboard.
3. Press **Enter** to submit. Use **Backspace** to remove a letter.
4. Use the clues to find the hidden word:
   - **Green**: the letter is correct and in the right place.
   - **Yellow**: the letter is in the word but in the wrong place.
   - **Gray**: the letter is not in the word.

## Game options

- **New word** starts a fresh, randomly chosen round.
- **Reset board** clears your guesses but keeps the same hidden word.
- **Statistics** shows your wins, streaks, win rate, and guess distribution for each word length.
- **Copy result** copies a shareable emoji summary after a round.
- Use the **moon icon** for dark mode and the **How to play** menu for high-contrast colors.

When you first open the game each day, each word length begins with that day’s curated word. Your current games, statistics, and preferences are saved in your browser.

## Game statistics

Statistics are stored locally in your browser, so no account is required. Each word length has its own totals, including games played, wins, win rate, current and best streaks, and guess distribution. Your stats stay on the same browser and device and can be cleared with **Reset all stats**.
